What signs can point to a hidden plumbing leak?

Damp spots, changing stains, or water sounds when fixtures aren’t running may be reasons to look closer. These signs don’t confirm a leak, so note what you observe and ask a plumber to assess it.

What should I do before a leak detection visit?

Write down where and when you noticed the problem, and keep the area accessible if it’s safe to do so. Don’t take apart plumbing or open a wall to investigate.

Should I call if the moisture comes and goes?

Yes, intermittent signs are still useful to discuss with a plumber. Tell them when the moisture appears and whether you’ve noticed any pattern.
